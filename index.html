About London

Welcome to my travel webpage about London. It is the capital city of England and one of the most famous cities in the world. It is a great place to visit because it has a long history, beautiful buildings, famous landmarks, museums, parks and many different restaurants. There are many interesting places to explore, whether you are visiting for a weekend or staying for several weeks.

Famous Places in London

Big Ben

Big Ben is one of London's most famous landmarks. The name Big Ben originally refers to the Great Bell inside the Elizabeth Tower. The tower was completed in 1859 and is part of the Palace of Westminster.

Tower Bridge

Tower Bridge was built between 1886 and 1894. It crosses the River Thames near the Tower of London and is famous for its two large towers and its lifting roadway, which allows ships to pass underneath.

Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace has been the London residence of the British monarch since 1837. It was originally built as Buckingham House in the early eighteenth century and was later expanded into the palace we see today.
